Core Empirical Findings

1. The Sleep Displacement Mechanism The ratio of daily screen time to sleep hours accounts for 48.6% of the variance in student GPA regression models. Students spending more hours on screens than in bed consistently register GPAs under 3.00.
2. Platform Stress Variation (ANOVA p < 0.05) Algorithmic short-video platforms (TikTok, Instagram) are associated with higher perceived stress scores and late-night doomscrolling frequency compared to informational feeds (YouTube, LinkedIn).
3. Non-Linear Bedtime Penalties Late-night phone usage correlates with a 2-point median decline in Sleep Quality Score, dampening cognitive consolidation regardless of daytime academic study hours.
